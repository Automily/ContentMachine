# Pip the Little Cloud — Ep. 01: "Find the Rainbow!"

**Format:** YouTube Short · 9:16 vertical · ~50 seconds · 9 scenes
**Audience:** Ages 2–5 (Made for Kids)
**Learning goal:** Name six colors (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple)
**Hook mechanic:** Each color Pip finds adds a stripe to a rainbow trailing behind it. The full rainbow is the payoff.

---

## Character & Style Bible (paste into every scene prompt)

**Pip:** a small, round, fluffy white cloud with big friendly brown eyes, rosy pink cheeks, a tiny happy smile, two little puffy cloud arms, and a soft warm glow around its edges.

**Style:** soft 3D storybook animation, rounded shapes, bright pastel colors, gentle sunny lighting, clean uncluttered backgrounds, cheerful and calm mood, no text in the image, vertical 9:16 framing.

**Negative prompt (if supported):** scary, dark, sharp teeth, angry, realistic humans, text, watermark, logo, blurry, distorted face, extra eyes, flashing lights.

**Voice:** warm, friendly narrator. Slow and clear, about 110 words per minute. Leave real pauses where marked `(pause)` so kids can shout the answer.

**Music:** gentle ukulele and glockenspiel loop, low volume under the voice.

**SFX:** a soft sparkly *chime* each time a rainbow stripe appears.

---

## Scene 1: Hook (0:00–0:05)

**Visual prompt:**
Pip [character bible] pops into view in the center of a clear blue sky with small puffs of sparkle, waving hello at the camera with one puffy arm. [style]

**Camera:** quick gentle zoom-in on Pip, then hold.
**Narration:** "Hi friends! I'm Pip! Can you help me find the colors of the rainbow?"
**On-screen text:** Find the colors! 🌈
**SFX:** pop + sparkle

---

## Scene 2: RED (0:05–0:11)

**Visual prompt:**
Pip [character bible] floats beside a leafy green apple tree in a sunny garden, looking curiously at one big shiny red apple hanging on a branch. A glowing red stripe of light swirls out of the apple and attaches behind Pip like the start of a rainbow ribbon. [style]

**Camera:** slow pan from Pip to the apple, then back to Pip with the red stripe.
**Narration:** "Look! A big, round apple. What color is it? (pause) RED!"
**On-screen text:** RED 🍎 (in red)
**SFX:** chime

## Scene 3: ORANGE (0:11–0:17)

**Camera:** follow the butterfly in a gentle circle around Pip.
**Narration:** "A fluttery butterfly! What color? (pause) ORANGE!"
**On-screen text:** ORANGE 🦋 (in orange)

## Scene 8: Payoff (0:41–0:48)

**Visual prompt:**
Pip [character bible] cheers with both puffy arms raised as a big, complete rainbow (red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple) arcs across a bright blue sky behind Pip, with gentle sparkles. [style]

**Camera:** pull back to reveal the full rainbow.
**Narration:** "We did it! Red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple — a RAINBOW!"
**On-screen text:** 🌈 RAINBOW! (each color word appearing in its color)
**SFX:** big happy sparkle + music swell

## Production Notes

- **Consistency:** keep the character bible text *identical* in every prompt. If EveryGen supports a character reference image, generate Pip once (Scene 1) and use that image as the reference for Scenes 2–9.
- **Rainbow continuity:** each scene adds exactly one stripe, in rainbow order. If the model struggles with the trailing stripes, drop them from the prompts and add the rainbow ribbon as a simple overlay in editing.
- **Pacing:** don't trim the `(pause)` beats. They make the video interactive and help with watch time.
- **Safety:** no flashing or strobing, no sudden loud sounds, no jump cuts. Calm and bright throughout.
